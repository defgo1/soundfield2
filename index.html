GPS troubleshooting for phones that cannot track

Use the full HTTPS website directly in Safari or Chrome (not an embedded preview), enable location services for that browser, allow the site to use precise location, then tap “Start location tracking” again. On iPhone inspect Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and Safari Websites / your browser; on Android inspect the browser's site permissions and system location permission. Some browsers do not support querying the permission state.

Field notes · Testing with 2–20+ phones

① Indoor rehearsal
Open on each phone, stay in manual mode, pick different musical parts, tap “Start clock now” and arm sound. Share a common link for closer alignment.
② Outdoor walk
On one phone, use your GPS position as centre, choose 80m, tap “Begin in 90 seconds”, then share the invitation. Everyone opens it, starts GPS and arms sound.
③ Hear the collective sound
Spread out, walk between quadrants, and listen to how the tones combine through the air. GPS accuracy and speaker loudness vary by device.
